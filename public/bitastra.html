OPEN-SOURCE FOUNDATION

Built from proven wallet technology.

BitAstra is an independent Bitcoin-focused browser-wallet derivative informed by the open-source CoinSpace wallet architecture and developed by NeuralNexusLab. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by CoinSpace. The included CoinSpace MIT notice is available in the repository.

BitAstra is a self-custody browser hot wallet. Use at your own risk; it is not a hardware wallet and no security claim can eliminate all risk.
