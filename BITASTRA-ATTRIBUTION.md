# BitAstra attribution and scope

BitAstra is an independent, Bitcoin-mainnet-only browser-wallet implementation developed by NeuralNexusLab. Its product flow and self-custody wallet architecture were informed by the official open-source CoinSpace project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by CoinSpace.

The CoinSpace MIT license and copyright notice are retained in `public/licenses/COINSPACE-MIT.txt`. BitAstra does not ship Coin.Space’s server-assisted PIN/token flow, exchange, ramps, or blockchain backend.

The original repository used for architectural reference is https://github.com/CoinSpace/CoinSpace.
