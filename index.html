Experiment protocol

Question everything. Ship something.

Our process is deliberately simple: investigate an interesting edge, build the smallest honest version, then learn from what happens outside the lab.

01Observe

Find the friction.

We look for technical problems people have accepted for too long and ask whether they can be made simpler.

02Prototype

Make it real.

We learn through code, assembling focused experiments that expose both the potential and the limits of an idea.

03Release

Open the loop.

We put useful work into the world, observe how it behaves, and keep evolving what earns a reason to exist.
